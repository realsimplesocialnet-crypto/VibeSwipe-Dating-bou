description: Owner changed VibeSwipes from a dating app to an Instagram-style social networking product.

The owner explicitly selected “Replace dating with social features” when asked whether the social conversion should be appearance-only or replace dating functionality.

**Why:** The owner requested converting the whole project to an Instagram-style social app, not keeping dating beneath a visual reskin.

**How to apply:** Treat social posting, following, feeds, Reels, Stories, profiles, and messaging as the product direction. Preserve existing data and safety behavior; do not interpret a redesign as permission to delete accounts or content. Keep MyAgeBeauty as the product brand and do not use Instagram or TikTok branding/assets.

The owner later confirmed the app name is MyAgeBeauty and asked for the same social experience on Android and web.

**Why:** The owner corrected the app's name and said the mobile and website should match.

**How to apply:** Keep MyAgeBeauty branding and the social navigation/visual language consistent across the existing Android and web apps. Do not add “Limited” to the product name; reserve the legal entity name for legal disclosures.
