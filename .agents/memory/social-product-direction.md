name: Social networking product direction
description: Owner changed VibeSwipes from a dating app to an Instagram-style social networking product.
---

The owner explicitly selected “Replace dating with social features” when asked whether the Instagram-style conversion should be appearance-only or replace dating functionality.

**Why:** The owner requested converting the whole project to an Instagram-style social app, not keeping dating beneath a visual reskin.

**How to apply:** Treat social posting, following, feeds, Reels, Stories, profiles, and messaging as the product direction. Preserve existing data during conversion; do not interpret the request as permission to delete accounts or content. Retain VibeSwipes branding rather than impersonating Instagram.
