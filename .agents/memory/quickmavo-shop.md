Quickmavo.com is the user's other website for phone numbers, followers, eSIM, airtime, and other digital services.

Keep its optional shop entry in Settings. Do not promote it on Discover or chat, auto-open it, or add unsolicited banners/popups. Ask “You are about to leave this app and go to Quickmavo.com. Continue?” with Cancel and Continue before navigating. Prefer an in-app browser on installed apps; browser visits must preserve an easy return to VibeSwipe.

**Why:** The user wants access to their other services without interrupting or pressuring dating-app users.

**How to apply:** Treat the link as a voluntary Settings action, not an onboarding step, ad interruption, or required redirect.
