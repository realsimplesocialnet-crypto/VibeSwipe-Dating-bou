---
name: Main-screen headers
description: User's preference for compact branding and reduced top space on the main tabs.
---

Keep the top logo and wordmark small and the shared header compact on all main screens.

**Why:** The user wants the app to feel like Instagram, with more room for stories, cards, and titles rather than a large logo area.

**How to apply:** Preserve this compact approach when changing main-tab headers.

Keep Discover's branding, Stories, swipe card, actions, and bottom navigation within one screen. Do not let the swipe card scroll underneath Stories. When a swipe card is present, put the user's own story entry in the labelled Stories row, not as a separate personal avatar beside the branding.

**Why:** The user clarified with two screenshots that the card moving underneath the sticky Stories area was cutting off the head and hiding the action buttons. A fixed photo ratio alone did not fix document scrolling.

**How to apply:** Fit the photo into the remaining space after the real header, install banner, actions, and navigation. Keep this Discover-only screen containment without affecting scrolling on other screens.
